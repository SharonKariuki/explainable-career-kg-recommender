# Participant information sheet

**Study title:** Testing AjiraGuide, a career guidance tool for IT and computer-science students

Draft for ethics committee review. It must not be given to students until the committee has approved it.

## Who is doing this study

[Principal investigator], [position], [institution]. Supervisor: [Supervisor]. This study has been reviewed by [Strathmore University IREC or equivalent], reference [approval number].

## What is the study about

AjiraGuide is a web app that suggests careers based on the skills a student types in. We want to know whether its suggestions make sense, whether its explanations are clear, and whether it is easy to use. We are testing the tool, not you.

## Why you are invited

You are an IT or computer-science student, and your view will help improve the tool.

## Do you have to take part

No. Taking part is voluntary. If you say no, or stop later, nothing happens: no effect on your marks, your lecturers, or your studies. You do not have to give a reason. You can withdraw at any time, including during the session, without penalty.

## What will happen

One session of about 40 minutes with a researcher. You will:

1. type in your own skills;
2. look at the careers suggested and rate how relevant the top three are to you;
3. read one explanation and answer three short questions about it;
4. look at your skills gap, the career list and your history;
5. answer a short usability questionnaire and one open question;
6. at the end, if you want, delete your account and data from the app.

## What data we collect

Your skills list, your ratings and answers, your questionnaire scores, notes about where you got stuck, and your year of study and programme. A careers adviser will also grade the same skills list without knowing what the app suggested. We do not store your name with your answers. You get a code (for example P01) instead.

## How your data is kept

Responses are stored under your code only, on [storage location], accessible only to the research team. Your signed consent form is kept separately [where]. Responses and consent forms will be deleted by [date]. Results will be reported only as group figures, and no one will be identifiable. We follow the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

## Possible risks and benefits

The risks are small: some tiredness, or feeling unsure about your career options. The tool gives guidance only. It is not a promise of a job or a place anywhere, and it can be wrong. Careers service contact: [contact]. There is no payment [or: refreshments/allowance as approved by the committee]. The benefit is helping improve a tool that may help future students.
