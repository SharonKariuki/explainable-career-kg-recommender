Privacy policy

AjiraGuide · last updated 12 November 2026

AjiraGuide suggests technology careers from the skills you enter and explains why. It is a final-year student project at Strathmore University. This page explains what it stores about you and why.

What we store

  • Your account: a username, your email address, and a securely hashed password if you set one.
  • If you sign in with Google: your email address and Google's account identifier, so the same Google account signs you back in. We do not receive your Google password, contacts or any other Google data.
  • What you enter: your skills, the careers you save and the skills you mark as in progress.
  • In your browser: a sign-in token and your light or dark theme choice, so you stay signed in and the page looks the way you left it. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

Only to run AjiraGuide: to sign you in, recommend careers, explain them and show the skills you could build next. We do not sell your information, share it with advertisers or use it for anything else.

Where it is kept

The app runs on Microsoft Azure and stores its data in a Neon Postgres database. Connections are encrypted. The site itself is served by Vercel, and fonts load from Google Fonts.

Deleting your data

Open Settings and choose Delete account. Your account, skills, saved careers and progress are removed permanently straight away.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
